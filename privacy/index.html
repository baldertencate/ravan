Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Ravân does not require an account. Your learning progress and preferences are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Ravân.

Information stored on your device

Ravân stores exercise progress, mastery information, settings, and reminder preferences in local app or browser storage. This information remains on your device. Resetting the app or clearing its storage removes it.

Android app

The Google Play version does not contain third-party analytics. If you enable practice reminders, Ravân requests notification permission and schedules notifications locally on your phone. Notification choices and learning progress are not transmitted to the author.

Website analytics

The web version uses Umami Cloud to understand aggregate usage. Umami does not use cookies or collect names, email addresses, or account information. It records page visits, selected app events, browser and device type, language, screen size, and approximate location. An IP address is used transiently to derive location and an anonymous session hash but is not stored.

External services

Ravân can open external services when you deliberately choose to share the app, contact the author, add a web calendar event, or make a voluntary contribution. Those services—including your email or calendar provider and PayPal—process information under their own privacy policies.

Sharing and sale of data

Ravân does not sell personal data. Ravân does not share learning progress with advertisers or other third parties. The app contains no advertising SDKs.

Children

Ravân is a general language-learning tool and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
